# QForge 1.7.1

## Changes

- **Network and NDI® outputs send what QForge plays, not your screen.** The picture now comes straight from QForge's own rendering of each output. Nothing on your desktop is shared. macOS no longer asks for Screen Recording, and output windows can be covered without affecting what's sent. Stage Manager's and Output's *Send this window as an NDI® source* work the same way.
- **Virtual outputs.** In **Settings ▸ Video outputs**, tick **Virtual output** to play an output at a fixed size (720p, 1080p, 1440p, 4K or portrait 1080×1920) without putting it on any display. Send it over NDI® or to QForge Output, and watch it in the program monitor.
- **Live video player on the stage manager screen.**
  - **QForge Stage Manager:** press Esc and choose *Video player ▸ Show "Main output"* (or any output). Tick *Player sound* if you want audio.
  - **Browser SM screen:** press **▶ Video** (bottom left) or **P**.
  - Drag the player's title bar to move it, drag its corner to resize it, and switch outputs from its menu.
  - Network outputs must be on in QForge (Settings ▸ Remote & network ▸ Network displays).
